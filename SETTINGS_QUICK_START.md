## 📞 Contact Section (NEW!)

### What It Shows
- 📧 **Email** - Clickable mailto link
- 📞 **Phone** - Clickable tel link
- 📍 **Address** - Full physical address
- 🕐 **Hours** - Business hours

### How to Configure
1. Go to **Settings → Contact** tab
2. Fill in:
   - Contact Title: "Get in Touch"
   - Contact Subtitle: "We'd love to hear from you"
   - Contact Email: "contact@yourstore.com"
   - Contact Phone: "+91 98765 43210"
   - Contact Address: "123 Silk Street, Mumbai"
   - Business Hours: "Mon-Sat: 10 AM - 7 PM"
3. Click **"Save All Settings"**

### Where It Appears
- Bottom of shop page (before footer)
- Only shows if at least one contact field is filled
- Responsive 1-4 column layout

## 🦶 Footer (NEW!)

### What It Shows
- **Column 1:** Logo, name, about text, social icons
- **Column 2:** Quick links (Home, Shop, About, Contact)
- **Column 3:** Customer service links (from settings)
- **Column 4:** Payment methods (COD, Card, UPI)
- **Bottom:** Copyright text

### How to Configure
1. Go to **Settings → Footer** tab
2. Fill in:
   - About Text: "Brief description of your store"
   - Copyright: "© 2026 Your Store. All rights reserved."
   - Footer Links: "Privacy Policy,Terms of Service,Shipping Policy"
3. Click **"Save All Settings"**

### Where It Appears
- Bottom of shop page
- Always visible
- Responsive 1-4 column layout

## 💡 Tips

### Contact Section
- Use professional email
- Include country code in phone
- Write complete address
- Use clear hours format

### Footer
- Keep about text concise
- Include year in copyright
- Use comma-separated links
- Add all active social profiles

### Images
- Logo: 200x200px square
- Banner: 1920x400px landscape
- Max file size: 5MB
- Formats: PNG, JPG

### Social Media
- Use full URLs (https://...)
- Test links before saving
- Include all active profiles
- WhatsApp: use country code
